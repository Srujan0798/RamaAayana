# Ramayana Game Plan (Combined)

## 1) Vision & Goals
- **Game vision:** A high-end, story-driven Ramayana experience grounded in the Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, honoring chronology, symbolism, and dharma without fantasy distortion.
- **Primary goal:** Narrative-first action-adventure with deliberate combat and spiritual realism.
- **Target audience:** Players seeking a respectful, cinematic, and contemplative adventure on mobile.
- **Success criteria:** A polished vertical slice (Prologue) meeting the quality bar and mobile performance targets.

## 2) Core Pillars
- **Narrative authenticity:** Canonical, minimal dialogue, silence used intentionally.
- **Gameplay loop:** Explore → observe → encounter → restrained combat → reflection.
- **Visual style:** Semi-stylized realism; painterly, spiritual, cinematic.
- **Tone:** Serene → heroic → tragic → transcendent.

## 3) Game Overview
- **Genre:** Third-person action-adventure with narrative exploration.
- **Perspective:** God-of-War-style close third-person, cinematic cuts.
- **Platforms:** Android (primary). Optional iOS after vertical slice passes.
- **Controls:** Virtual joystick (left), context buttons (right), one action = one intention.

## 4) Story & Setting
- **Scope of the Ramayana:** Canonical arcs focused on Rama and Hanuman; no mythology fusion.
- **Key story arcs:**
  - Prologue: Ayodhyā at dawn (child Rama)
  - Act I: Exile (Chitrakūṭa, Daṇḍakāraṇya)
  - Act II: Search (Sugrīva alliance, Hanuman introduction)
  - Act III: Lankā (Hanuman stealth/infiltration)
  - Act IV: War (controlled spectacle, no gore)
  - Epilogue: Return (silence and rain)
- **Primary protagonist(s):** Rama (Act I), Hanuman (Act III), story-locked switching.
- **Key antagonists:** Rākṣasas, Rāvaṇa (kept restrained and canonical in depiction).
- **Supporting characters:** Sītā, Lakṣmaṇa, Sugrīva, Hanumān, Daśaratha, Kaikeyī.
- **World locations:** Ayodhyā, Chitrakūṭa, Daṇḍakāraṇya, Kiṣkindhā, Lankā, battlefield.

## 5) Characters & Progression
- **Playable characters (sequential):**
  - **Rama (Act I):** Calm, restrained combat, bow mastery, dharma-based choices.
  - **Hanuman (Act III):** Speed, strength, devotion-powered abilities; parkour, stealth, aerial combat.
- **Progression system:** Story-locked; abilities unlock with narrative events.

## 6) Gameplay Systems
- **Combat system:** Skill-based, deliberate, stamina-driven; enemies punish button-mashing; bosses test restraint.
- **Dharma system (core):** Invisible moral meter; actions affect world tone, NPC reactions, and music (no UI numbers).
- **Exploration:** Sacred spaces reward silence; stillness restores color; violence desaturates the world.
- **Quests/Missions:** No fetch quests; every objective serves story or dharma.

## 7) Art & Audio Direction
- **Global art consistency:** Transitions are gradual; color grading tells the story.
- **Mood sequence (fixed order):**
  1. **Soft Dawn Watercolor (Ayodhyā / Child Rama):** ivory, gold, pale saffron, sky blue; diffused sunrise; soft edges.
  2. **Forest Mist Oil-Paint (Exile / Search):** muted greens/browns; volumetric fog; visible brush strokes.
  3. **Temple Sculpture Realism (Lankā / War / Epilogue):** stone gray, crimson, obsidian; hard light, deep shadow.
- **Character design:** Classical sculpture + realism; no exaggerated muscles or glowing effects unless scripturally justified.
- **Audio style:** Sanskrit shlokas as ambient layers; dynamic score reacts to dharma state; silence used intentionally.
- **Voiceover/localization:** Minimal dialogue; subtitles mandatory.

## 8) MVP Scope (First Playable)
- **Playable slice:** Prologue (Ayodhyā walk), one forest combat encounter, one cinematic.
- **Key features included:**
  - Third-person camera and mobile controls
  - Dharma system feedback (color/audio shift)
  - Basic combat loop and stamina
  - Cinematic pipeline for prologue
- **Out of scope for MVP:** Lankā stealth, large-scale war, full progression.

## 9) Production Plan
- **Phase 1 (Vertical Slice):** Prologue + one forest combat + one cinematic. If this fails, project stops.
- **Phase 2 (Act I – Rama):** Exile, forest hubs, dharma system fully implemented.
- **Phase 3 (Act II–III – Hanuman):** Movement-heavy gameplay, Lankā stealth mission.
- **Phase 4 (War + Epilogue):** Scaled battle, controlled spectacle, silent ending.
- **Team roles needed:** Game Director, Narrative Designer, Level Designer, Art Director, Gameplay Engineer, Audio Designer, QA Lead.
- **Estimated timeline:** TBD after vertical slice scope lock.

## 10) Technical Requirements (Locked)
- **Engine:** Unity (URP) for mobile stability and performance.
- **Performance budget:** 30–45 FPS stable on Android.
- **Level structure:** Semi-open hubs; hidden loading via forest turns, cave entry, cinematic pauses.
- **Save points:** Narrative rests only.

## 11) Canonical Opening Flow (Confirmed)
1. **Silent playable Child Rama** in Ayodhyā at dawn (movement + observation only).
2. **Time skip cinematic** with bow ceremony glimpse (non-playable).
3. **Adult Rama**; exile begins; core mechanics unlock slowly.

## 12) Scene-by-Scene Cinematic Scripts (Condensed)
- **Scene 0 — Ayodhyā at Dawn (Playable Cinematic):** Child Rama walks; guard bows; flower falls; he pauses. White fade.
- **Scene 1 — The Decree (Cinematic):** Daśaratha: “I gave my word.” Kaikeyī: “Then keep it.” Rama: “I will go.” Silence.
- **Scene 2 — Exile Departure (Playable → Cinematic):** Walk to gate; crowd parts; Lakṣmaṇa: “Where you go, I go.”
- **Scene 3 — Forest Years (Montage):** Camps, sages, seasons; wind + tanpura.
- **Scene 4 — Sītā Abduction (Cinematic):** Absence, silence, Rama kneels.
- **Scene 5 — Meeting Hanumān (Interactive):** Hanumān: “I am a servant.” Rama: “Stand. Walk with me.”
- **Scene 6 — Lankā Infiltration (Playable):** Hanumān sees Sītā; regains control.
- **Scene 7 — Burning of Lankā (Playable):** “This is not victory.”
- **Scene 8 — War (Cinematic + Gameplay):** Final arrow; no glory music.
- **Scene 9 — Return (Ending):** Rain, mud, silence; fade before coronation.

## 13) Art Prompts (Agent-Ready)
- **Ayodhyā Environment:** Semi-stylized ancient Indian city at dawn, soft watercolor lighting, ivory/gold palette, calm symmetry, painterly textures, mobile-optimized.
- **Forest Environment:** Dense Indian forest with morning mist, oil-paint texture, volumetric fog, muted greens/browns, sacred atmosphere.
- **Lankā Environment:** Temple-sculpted fortress, obsidian stone, crimson accents, heavy shadows, restrained lighting.
- **Rama Character:** Calm warrior prince, restrained posture, simple dhoti and bow, classical proportions, serene expression.
- **Hanumān Character:** Humble devoted warrior, controlled build, minimal ornaments, respectful depiction.

## 14) Quality Bar (Non-Negotiable)
- No fetch quests.
- No mythology fusion.
- No power fantasy that disrespects humility.
- Every mechanic must serve story or dharma.

1. Full game design document.

3. Combat system spec.
4. Art bible.
5. Audio plan.
6. Playable vertical slice (Prologue).
