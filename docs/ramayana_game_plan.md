# Ramayana Game Plan (Draft)

## 1) Vision & Goals
- **Game vision:** Build a complete, playable, high-end aesthetic game grounded in Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa with no fantasy distortion and strong respect for chronology, symbolism, and dharma.
- **Primary goal:** Story-first, dharma-driven action-adventure focused on restraint, humility, and consequence.
- **Target audience:** Players seeking narrative exploration and culturally respectful storytelling on mobile.
- **Success criteria:** Vertical slice (Prologue) meets quality bar; stable 30–45 FPS; minimal UI; story-locked progression.

## 2) Core Pillars
- **Narrative authenticity:** Canonical, minimal dialogue, silence as intentional storytelling.
- **Gameplay loop:** Explore → story event → deliberate combat/stealth → reflection.
- **Visual style:** Semi-stylized realism; painterly, spiritual realism (not cartoonish).
- **Tone:** Serene → heroic → tragic → transcendent.

## 3) Game Overview
- **Genre:** Third-person action-adventure + narrative exploration.
- **Perspective:** God-of-War-style close third person, cinematic cuts.
- **Platforms:** Android (primary), iOS optional after vertical slice.
- **Controls:** Virtual joystick (left) + context buttons (right); one action = one intention.

## 4) Story & Setting
- **Scope of the Ramayana:** Full journey with story-locked, canonical arc order.
- **Key story arcs:**
  - Prologue: Child Rāma in Ayodhyā at dawn
  - Act I: Exile, forest life, protection of sages
  - Act II: Search, alliance with Sugrīva, Hanumān introduced
  - Act III: Lankā infiltration (Hanumān arc)
  - Act IV: War
  - Epilogue: Return and silence
- **Primary protagonist(s):** Rāma (Act I) → Hanumān (Act III), no free swapping.
- **Key antagonists:** Rāvaṇa and rākṣasas (no spectacle-driven depiction).
- **Supporting characters:** Sītā, Lakṣmaṇa, Sugrīva, Daśaratha, Kaikeyī.
- **World locations:** Ayodhyā, Chitrakūṭa, Daṇḍakāraṇya, Kiṣkindhā, Lankā.

## 5) Characters & Progression
- **Playable characters:** Rāma (primary), Hanumān (unlocked for Act III).
- **Abilities/skills:**
  - **Rāma:** Bow mastery, calm/defensive combat, dharma-driven choices.
  - **Hanumān:** Speed, strength, devotion-powered movement, stealth, parkour.
- **Progression system:** Story-locked unlocks; no grinding before destiny events.

## 6) Gameplay Systems
- **Combat system:** Skill-based, deliberate, stamina-driven; enemies punish button-mashing.
- **Exploration:** Semi-open hubs; sacred spaces reward silence; stillness restores color.
- **Quests/Missions:** Narrative-only; no fetch quests; no mythology fusion.
- **Puzzles/Mini-games:** Minimal, tied to story context.
- **Crafting/Upgrades:** None unless story-justified.
- **Dharma System (core):** Invisible moral meter; affects environment tone, NPC reactions, and music with no numeric UI.

## 7) Art & Audio Direction
- **Art style:** Semi-stylized realism with three fixed mood sequences:
  1. **Soft dawn watercolor (Ayodhyā):** ivory/gold, diffused sunrise, soft edges.
  2. **Forest mist oil-paint (Exile/Search):** deep greens/browns, volumetric fog.
  3. **Temple sculpture realism (Lankā/War/Epilogue):** stone gray/crimson/obsidian, heavy shadows.
- **Character design:** Classical sculpture proportions; no exaggerated muscles/glows unless scripturally justified.
- **Environment design:** Strong silhouettes, painterly textures, mobile-optimized geometry.
- **Audio style:** Sanskrit shlokas as low-volume ambient layers; dynamic score reacts to dharma; intentional silence after major events.
- **Voiceover/localization:** Minimal dialogue; subtitles mandatory.

## 8) MVP Scope (First Playable)
- **Playable slice:** Prologue (Ayodhyā walk) + one forest combat + one cinematic.
- **Key features included:**
  - Dharma system feedback (visual/audio)
  - Third-person mobile controls
  - Scene 0 playable cinematic
- **Out of scope for MVP:** Full act progression, open-world scale, war sequence.

## 9) Production Plan
- **Milestones:**
  1. **Phase 1 — Vertical Slice:** Prologue, one forest combat, one cinematic (project stops if this fails).
  2. **Phase 2 — Act I (Rāma):** Exile, forest hubs, dharma system.
  3. **Phase 3 — Act II–III (Hanumān):** Movement-heavy gameplay, Lankā stealth.
  4. **Phase 4 — War + Epilogue:** Scaled battle, controlled spectacle, silent ending.
- **Team roles needed:** Game Director, Narrative Designer, Level Designer, Art Director, Gameplay Engineer, Audio Designer, QA Lead.
- **Estimated timeline:** TBD after vertical slice scope validation.

## 10) Scene-by-Scene Cinematic Outline (Canon)
0. **Ayodhyā at dawn (playable):** Child Rāma walks; guard bows; flower falls; Rāma observes.
1. **The decree (cinematic):** Daśaratha, Kaikeyī, Rāma speak minimally; silence.
2. **Exile departure (playable → cinematic):** Walk to gate; crowd parts; Lakṣmaṇa vows.
3. **Forest years (montage):** Camps, sages, seasons; no dialogue.
4. **Sītā abduction (cinematic):** Absence, Rāma kneels; no tears.
5. **Meeting Hanumān (interactive dialogue):** Humility, service.
6. **Lankā infiltration (playable stealth):** Sees Sītā; regains control.
7. **Burning of Lankā (forced-path):** “This is not victory.”
8. **War (controlled cinematic + gameplay):** Rāma releases final arrow; no glory music.
9. **Return (ending):** Rain, silence, fade before coronation.

## 11) Technical Requirements
- **Engine:** Unity (URP) — fixed.
- **Performance:** 30–45 FPS stable; stylized realism over photorealism.
- **Level structure:** Semi-open hubs; linear story flow; hidden loading via turns/caves/cinematics.
- **Save points:** Only at narrative rests.
- **Input:** Controller + keyboard support where applicable; mobile primary.

## 12) Quality Bar (Non-Negotiable)
- No fetch quests.
- No mythology fusion.
- No power fantasy disrespecting humility.
- Every mechanic must serve story or dharma.
